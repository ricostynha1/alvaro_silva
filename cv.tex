\cvsection{Projects}
\begin{itemize}
 \item \textbf{Current investigation:} 

I am pursuing my Ph.D. under the supervision of Professor Alexandra Mendes from the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto, with co-supervision from Professor Ruben Martins from Carnegie Mellon University.

I am investigating ways to apply recent advances in artificial intelligence to enhance the usability of verification-aware languages, focusing on Dafny. I have already published work on this subject. I am also exploring other usability improvements for Dafny, such as better proof visualizations.  I plan to use this usability improvements to explore Dafny as a platform for hardware modeling and verification as a new application domain for the language.

 \item \textbf{VeriFixer: Automated Repair of Verification-Aware Languages:} I am a researcher on this project that intends to develops an open-source tool to automatically fix code issues identified by software verifiers, starting with the Dafny language. Funded through a competitive FCT grant and involving INESC TEC, INESC ID, and Carnegie Mellon University, it aims to bridge the gap between error detection and correction, reducing manual effort in building reliable software for critical domains.
 
    \item \textbf{MIPI Camera Controllers:} I was the verification lead engineer on the new CSI2 camera controllers. The project demands high competences in creating reliable and extensive verification environments.

    The environments model the Device under test (DUT) and stimulate it with all kinds of constrained randomized traffic. I also wrote a lot of temporal assertions and applied formal methods to small parts of the design when stronger behaviour assurances are needed.

    I have extensive knowledge in MIPI specifications and to convert them to assertions modules.
    

    \item \textbf{Wind Flow:} I was a \textbf{team leader} in a university project of \textbf{40 people}. My team (10 members) was responsible for implementing a communication wireless physical protocol using an SDR (software-defined radio). The project was a mesh of high-speed wind sensors that communicate wirelessly through long distances. 
    
    
    \item \textbf{Research Paper graph:} I was a member of a three-member team. The project consisted of a GUI Web-based application exposing research papers in graph structures and clustering papers by similarity. The details can be seen in \underline{
    \href{https://github.com/Jibbow/research-paper-graph}{research-paper-graph}}. I was responsible for doing the \textbf{AI clustering} distances between nodes which are plotted for the different publications. 
    
\end{itemize}
